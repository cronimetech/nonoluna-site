Nonoluna
A calm, cozy nonogram game for iPhone and iPad: solve each grid with logic and a hidden picture appears.

Fact sheet

PlatformsiPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later)
PriceFree download. Every puzzle is free to play.
CategoryGames › Puzzle
LanguagesEnglish, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian
PrivacyData Not Collected: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Works offline.
Websitecronimetech.github.io/nonoluna-site

Key features

  • Pure logic, no guessing. Every puzzle is checked to have exactly one solution you can reach by reasoning alone. Most yield to row-and-column logic; harder boards ask for careful “what if” reasoning, which is still logic, never luck. No timers; gentle hearts forgive a slip.
  • A daily ritual. A new daily puzzle every morning, with a monthly calendar to catch up on missed days.
  • A cozy Journey. Chapters of hidden pictures from 5×5 up to 15×15, with stars, a chest and a boss puzzle in every chapter.
  • Big Pictures. Solve 4 or 9 puzzles to reveal one large poster in full color.
  • Seasons through the year. Eight seasonal events, each with ten pictures: three to start, then a new one each day.
  • Accessible. VoiceOver reads every board cell and clue in all 10 languages; text follows Dynamic Type.
